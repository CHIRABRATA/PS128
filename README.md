# 🧠 AI-Powered Institutional Company Memory

> An AI-powered memory system that preserves a company's knowledge and helps employees get evidence-backed answers about past decisions, projects, people, and technical changes.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

In modern companies, important knowledge is scattered across Slack, GitHub, documents, meetings, and individual employees' experience.

When experienced employees leave, valuable **tribal knowledge** can disappear, making it difficult for new developers to understand **why decisions were made, who worked on something, or what happened in the past**.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

We are building an **AI-Powered Institutional Company Memory System** that collects, organizes, searches, and explains a company's existing knowledge.

Instead of simply asking an AI to generate an answer, our system:

```text
Company Knowledge
       ↓
Data Collection
       ↓
Knowledge Processing
       ↓
Company Memory
       ↓
Employee Question
       ↓
Relevant Evidence Retrieval
       ↓
RAG + LLM
       ↓
Answer + Original Sources
